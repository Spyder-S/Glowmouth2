# GlowMouth - Product Truth and Editorial Source

## Positioning

GlowMouth is an emerging AI-powered oral-health project exploring how digital imaging and image analysis could help people better understand changes in their oral health between professional appointments.

This website is a project and early-access presentation, not a claim of clinical availability or diagnostic certification.

## The problem to communicate

Oral-health changes can be hard for people to observe or interpret between professional checkups. People need approachable explanations, a sense of continuity, and reasons to consult dental professionals when appropriate.

## Product experience - verify against actual assets before publishing

An envisioned experience may include a handheld oral-imaging device concept, a companion app, illustrative AI-supported image observations, and a sample 0-100 `GlowScore` showing trends over time. These are concepts from the current redesign brief, not independently verified shipping features.

Never claim users can buy, scan with, or receive diagnoses from an unverified prototype.

If the actual project turns out to be software-only or has a different physical form, change the visuals to match verified reality. Do not invent a hardware product.
