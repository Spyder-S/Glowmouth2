Privacy

GlowMouth is pre-launch. The only thing this site collects is what you type into the waitlist form, and this page describes all of it.

What we store

  • Your email address.
  • Your first name, if you chose to give one. It is optional.
  • The date and time you signed up.

That is the whole record. We do not run advertising trackers, we do not build a profile on you, and we do not sell or share this list.

What we use it for

To email you occasional GlowMouth updates and to tell you when the product becomes available. Nothing else.

Where it lives

In a private database hosted by Supabase. Submissions are written by a server function; the list is not publicly readable.

Removing yourself

Email hello@glowmouth.org and ask. We will delete your record and confirm when it is done. You do not need to give a reason.

Changes

If what we collect changes, this page changes with it before the change takes effect.
